# Penguini bakes brown butter chocolate chip cookies

**Status — 🔵 Ready for the first animation increment.** This storyboard follows the landing-page kitchen under NOMS-013. The current 45.2-second loop already has an excited jump, a recipe-book reading pose, ingredient pours, and mixing. Use those motions where they fit the new order. The [kitchen model notes](../../assets/models/penguini-cookie-kitchen/README.md) describe the editable Blender scene and its web export.

## First increment — from the welcome jump to melting butter

1. Open on the familiar excited jump. Let Penguini land and settle his weight before he reaches for the butter. A lit oven indicator establishes that the oven is preheated.
2. Have him look at the pat, pick it up from its dish, and carry it over the pot on a medium-low burner. Keep the pat in contact with his flipper until he releases it over the pot.
3. Show the pat beginning to melt. End with the butter in the pot, the dish empty, and his flipper relaxed as his gaze turns toward the recipe book.

Review this short passage on its own before animating the first book read. From the landing-page camera, including on a phone, the viewer should be able to follow the same pat from dish to pot and see where Penguini will look next.
