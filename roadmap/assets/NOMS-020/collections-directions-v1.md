# Collections explorations v1

Five proposals generated with the built-in image generation tool; each shows a collection library and opened owner-view collection. No direction is selected. No app code changed.

## Review notes

All proposals retain the cozy cream/sage/coral and gentle neumorphic direction. Exact Fredoka/Nunito typography and approved A3 logo must replace generated approximations. Shared navigation is intended to follow selected N1/N4; generated bars are flatter and less inset than the selected silhouette, not a new navigation decision. Counts, text, illustrations and metadata are illustrative and sometimes inconsistent. Correct “New collections” to “New collection”. Share controls must respect visibility and permissions; a private collection's link does not grant access. Cover themes, collage generation, search, grid/list toggles and preview panes are exploratory treatments, not commitments to new behavior. Resolve against current collection functionality before implementation.

C1 emphasizes notebook covers; C2 illustrated recipe boxes; C3 photo collages; C4 local hierarchy and preview; C5 compact readable rows. C4's sidebar is local collection navigation, not replacement shared navigation. Empty/loading/error states, viewer/non-owner variants, reorder accessibility, edit/delete impact, long paths/names, missing covers, dark mode and phone/tablet remain to be designed.

## C1 · Recipe Notebook Library

[View both examples](collections-c1-v1.png)

### Generation prompt

Use case: ui-mockup. Generate a polished landscape NOMS concept board with TWO large desktop page examples side by side, LEFT "Collection library" and RIGHT "Opened collection", readable clear UI. Cozy cute personal recipe-journal identity, warm buttery cream, soft sage/olive and muted coral. Rounded Fredoka display headings, Nunito labels/body, gentle neumorphic raised cards/buttons and inset search, thin borders and strong dark olive readable text. Very subtle paper texture, no excessive slogans or decorations. SAME selected N1/N4 shared navigation in BOTH examples: slightly inset floating rounded cream horizontal bar, SMALL tilted olive book logo with cream NOMS label and coral bookmark, coral Noms wordmark, Dashboard / Collections / Explore, sage active Collections pill, compact Search, profile icon. No global sidebar. Library heading "Your collections", subcopy "Little homes for the recipes you love.", coral "New collection" button, collection search. Three named collections "Weeknight favourites", "Weekend baking", "Family recipes", counts explicitly illustrative. Opened collection breadcrumb "Collections / Weeknight favourites", title, short description, private visibility label, clearly labelled Edit, Share, Add recipes and Reorder controls (owner view). Separate "Child collections" section with "One-pot dinners" and "Quick lunches", then "Recipes" with "Tomato pasta" and "Sunday soup". Keep nested collections visually distinct from recipes. All controls horizontal and legible. Do not invent meal plans, shopping, social likes or collaborative editing. This is a usable screen design, not a physical product photograph. Board title "C1 · Recipe Notebook Library". Specific direction: Library: three raised cards featuring charming illustrated closed recipe notebooks, cream title plates, small food preview thumbnails and recipe counts. Opened collection: modest notebook cover at heading, child collections as small notebook cards and recipes in a clear grid. Do not render a physical bookshelf or oversized open book.
