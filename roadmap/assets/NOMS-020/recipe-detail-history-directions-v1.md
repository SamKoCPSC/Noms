# Recipe detail and history explorations v1

Ten desktop pairs generated with the built-in image generation tool, 2026-09-13. Left Recipe Detail, right History. No direction selected; no app code changed. Phone/tablet remain deferred.

## Design boundaries

Visual QA: all ten pairs are generated. R2/R3/R8 introduce blue selection styling; use the approved palette in refinement. R3 gives drafts a warning-like red treatment; private does not mean error. R4's “All changes are saved” is too broad, and R5's “visible to everyone” must not generalize publishing to public visibility. R9's diff annotations and ingredient snapshots are inconsistent; use actual version data and explicit comparison direction. R10's fork must be explicitly identified as a separate recipe with attribution, not merely a generic branch. Dates, counts and recipe copy vary. Several concepts overemphasize Restore or duplicate History access; refine hierarchy and preserve confirmation. Ingredient icons are optional illustration proposals, not an approved icon-generation feature. None of the images replaces exact A3 artwork, shared navigation specification or permission checks.

Retain N1/N4 navigation, warm cream/sage/coral, Fredoka/Nunito and gentle neumorphism. Generated logos, fonts, data, dates, recipe quantities and wording are approximate. These are visual proposals, not new behavior requirements. Published versions must distinguish Current from Selected, private drafts stay owner-only and separate, restore creates a new version without erasing history, comparison targets must be explicit. NOMS-010 owns history/branching/comparison behavior. Forks are distinct recipe lineages, not arbitrary same-recipe branches or merge support. Public/non-owner views must hide unauthorized editing, restore and draft actions. Existing share, collection membership, scaling and permissions remain authoritative.

Remaining: exact route/action mapping, full comparison UI, historical snapshots, restore confirmation, fork attribution, long/nested recipes, equipment/images, missing-photo and loading/error states, keyboard navigation, contrast, dense graph behavior and dark mode. No selected board is implementation-ready. Ingredients/steps and sample counts are illustrative, not a tested recipe.

Detail uses calm two-column ingredients/method layout with compact photo and a small chronological history strip. History uses clear vertical chronological rows and a selected-version summary alongside.

### Generation prompt

Use case: ui-mockup. ONE high-fidelity landscape NOMS concept board, TWO readable desktop screens side by side: LEFT "Recipe detail", RIGHT "History". No phones/tablets. Warm cozy cute adult personal recipe-journal style, buttery cream, sage/olive, muted coral, Fredoka rounded headings and Nunito labels/body. Gentle neumorphic raised cream controls and inset wells, subtle paper texture, thin borders and dark readable olive text. No glassmorphism, ratings, likes, comments, shopping or meal planning, excessive slogans or decorative room props.
Both screens identical selected N1/N4 navigation: slightly inset floating rounded cream horizontal bar, small A3 tilted closed olive book with cream NOMS label/coral bookmark, coral Noms wordmark, Dashboard / Collections / Explore / Search / profile.
Same example recipe "Tomato pasta", author Alex Chen, owner view, Public, "v3 · Current", 30 min, 4 servings. Detail photo of tomato pasta, labelled Edit recipe, Add to collection, Share, History actions. Ingredients 400 g pasta, 600 g tomatoes, 3 cloves garlic, 2 tbsp olive oil, basil. Three concise method steps cook pasta / simmer sauce / toss and serve. Servings control, recipe note "Save a little pasta water." No active cooking timer or nutrition features.
History title "Tomato pasta · History". Published versions: v3 Current Sep 12 "Added basil"; v2 Selected Sep 10 "Reduced garlic"; v1 Sep 8 "First published". Every record clearly labelled Published. Controls View version, Compare v2 and v3, Restore v2 with helper "Restoring creates a new version; existing history stays." Restore must not look like immediate destructive deletion. Private drafts are not published versions: if any draft is shown, separate owner-only panel labelled Private draft, outside published timeline. Show readable selected versus current states. Dates and copy illustrative. No merging or collaborative permissions invented. Functional text horizontal. Board title "R1 · Kitchen Journal". History uses clear vertical chronological rows and a selected-version summary alongside.
