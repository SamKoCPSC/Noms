# NOMS-020 logo assets

The canonical reference is **S3 — Continuous olive**, the rightmost book in the [spine comparison sheet](spine-options-s1-s3-v1.png), selected 2026-09-27, replacing S1. See [selection, prompt and production caveats](spine-options-s1-s3-v1.md).

Preserve the tilted olive book, continuous rounded olive spine matching the cover with a subtle vertical hinge crease, cream pages and title label, coral strap/bookmark, smooth matte painted surfaces and crisp directional lighting. No horizontal spine bands or segmented blocks.

A standalone [static web SVG](../../../../assets/brand/noms-logo.svg) is available as of 2026-09-27, with transparent background, actual Fredoka 600 outlines and simplified vector shading. See [usage and validation](../../../../assets/brand/README.md) and [size/surface preview](../../../../assets/brand/preview.html). Page integration, favicon/single-colour variants and the 3D model remain pending.

[A3 — Tilted Label Book](noms-logo-planned-a3.png), [F2](painted-f2-smooth-sharp-v1.md) and S1 are superseded and retained as exploration history, alongside [painted P1–P3](painted-proposals-v1.md), [brushwork B1–B4](brushwork-variations-v1.md), [F1](painted-f1-dimensional-v1.md) and unselected S2. Earlier mockups may depict earlier marks; S3 governs future logo implementation. The eight selected page icons are unchanged; their references to S1 describe the original shared style, not a competing current logo selection.

The nine `logo-proposal-*.webp` files are earlier explorations, not selected alternatives. They are retained for history.

Artwork was moved here from the previous design-document location and the flat NOMS-020 asset folder; no images were modified.
