## Prompt set

Use case: style-transfer. Input image is edit target and reference for the NOMS A3 book logo, painted P3 brushwork. Produce one square proposal image, one large centered closed book on plain warm ivory. Preserve single book geometry and slight clockwise tilt/three-quarter perspective, olive green cover, ivory segmented left spine, visible ivory page block bottom/right, ivory rounded title plaque with crisp dark olive NOMS lettering, coral right-side elastic and bottom bookmark. Keep palette and recognizable design unchanged. Title exactly NOMS, friendly rounded sans resembling Fredoka; no serifs. Vary only the painting technique. Keep visibly painted contours and brush strokes on the book, not merely grain on background. No props, no faces, no additional symbols or books, no photoreal product render. Label below book with variant name. Treatment:

B1 — Sweeping strokes: long sweeping diagonal dry-brush marks, broad olive strokes and restrained sage highlights, confidently painted and cohesive.

B2 — Layered paint: short overlapping flat-brush strokes with tactile opaque pigment layers, subtle impasto, handmade storybook quality, matte not glossy.

B3 — Bristle texture: broken paint trails with distinct fine bristle grooves inside broad strokes, irregular painted contours, airy cream highlights, preserve strong solid silhouette.

B4 — Bold painterly: fewer larger decisive brush strokes and chunky color planes, highly readable silhouette, expressive handmade finish without tiny grain or distressed scratches.

## Review

All four preserve the closed-book composition, label, olive/ivory/coral palette and recognizable silhouette. B1 emphasizes long directional strokes; B2 layered pigment; B3 fine broken bristles; B4 broader simpler painted planes. Typography is generated and only approximates Fredoka. These are opaque raster concept images with proposal captions, not production SVG or 3D deliverables. Small-size readability still needs testing.
