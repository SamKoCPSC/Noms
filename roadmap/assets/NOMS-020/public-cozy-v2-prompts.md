# Cozy public-page proposals v2

Outputs: [Home](public-home-cozy-v2.png), [About](public-about-cozy-v2.png), [How to](public-how-to-cozy-v2.png).

Visual review: generated peripheral handwritten slogans are overabundant and should be reduced in implementation. Logo rendering is illustrative, not a replacement for approved A3. Home's "Save recipes from anywhere" is not a promise of URL import. How-to's "Get in touch" is not an approved new route. Private recipe visibility and private draft lifecycle must remain distinct. These are condensed page concepts, not complete content inventories.

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Exploratory mockups, not implementation or final copy. Typography is an approximation of Fredoka and Nunito. Builds on the clearer v1 layouts while restoring rounded coral headings, cream neumorphic controls, and restrained illustrated recipe-journal details.

## Home

Use case: ui-mockup. Generate a polished NOMS website design concept, one page per image, landscape desktop composition with header, hero and meaningful lower sections visible. Cozy personal recipe journal, cute but adult and usable. Warm buttery cream background, soft sage and olive, muted coral primary buttons and playful headings. Fredoka rounded display type and Nunito body type, never condensed or corporate. Strong original UI character: raised neumorphic cream cards and pill buttons with soft white upper-left highlights and gentle lower-right shadows, inset search fields, rounded edges. Maintain thin borders and strong readable dark olive text. Small hand-drawn kitchen illustrations, cheerful notebook details, a few paper-note accents. Mostly calm solid reading surfaces, very subtle paper texture. NOT a photorealistic marketing site; no food photography, no busy scrapbook clutter, no faces on every object. Header uses the approved small single closed olive notebook logo with cream title plate, coral elastic and bookmark, slight clockwise tilt, beside coral Noms wordmark. Navigation Home, Search, Explore, About, How to, Sign in. Actual page mockup viewed straight on, no device frame. HOME page. Headline "A cozy home for your recipes." Subcopy "Save the meals you love, keep your notes, and make each version your own." Left hero copy and inset search "Search recipes, collections, and cooks", raised coral "Start creating" and cream "Explore recipes" buttons. Right hero charming softly illustrated olive recipe notebook with coral bookmark, bowl of soup, wooden spoon, tiny herb sprig on a cream abstract blob. Below three tactile rounded cards "Capture", "Organize", "Make it yours" with small charming hand-drawn illustrations. Lower section "A little better, every time." with three neat recipe-version notes linked in a readable sequence, a small taped note "A little more garlic next time". Warm generous whitespace. Label upper margin "H2 · Cozy Home".
